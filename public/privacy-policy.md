# Privacy Policy

**Last updated March 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy for Jack Digilov (doing business as Real Labs) ("we," "us," or "our") describes how and why we collect, store, use, and share ("process") your information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:

- Download and use our mobile application (4Real), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Policy

**What information do we collect?** We collect the content you submit for factchecking, along with an anonymous user id. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information. There is no account creation or login.

**Do we process any sensitive personal information?** No.

**With whom do we share your information?** We share information derived from your submissions with third-party AI service providers to perform the factcheck analysis. We use cloud infrastructure providers to host and operate the Services. We do not sell or share your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

**How do we keep your information safe?** We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures. However, no system is 100% secure.

**What are your rights?** Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at robointern@reallabs.ai.

## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

**In Short:** We collect the content you submit and an anonymous user id. Nothing else.

When you use 4Real, we collect the following information:

- **Content you submit** — the material you share with the app for factchecking, such as links, text, or video. This is necessary to provide the service.
- **Anonymous user id** — a randomly generated ID used to associate your factcheck requests with your device so we can deliver results back to you. This id is not linked to your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, or any other personally identifying information.

We do **not** collect:

- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, or other device data
- Browsing history or activity outside the app
- Biometric data
- Financial or payment information

There is no account creation, login, or registration in 4Real. We do not require or store any credentials.

**Sensitive Information.** We do not process sensitive personal information.

## 2. How Do We Process Your Information?

**In Short:** We process your information to provide and improve the factchecking service.

We process your information for the following purposes:

- **To provide the factchecking service.** We process your submissions to deliver factcheck results to you.
- **To improve the service.** We retain submitted content and factcheck results to analyze the quality and accuracy of our factchecking, identify errors, and improve our systems over time.
- **To maintain and secure the service.** We may use technical information to monitor for abuse, prevent fraud, and ensure the stability and security of our infrastructure.
- **To comply with legal obligations.** We may process your information where required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request from a government authority.

## 3. What Legal Bases Do We Rely On to Process Your Information?

**In Short:** We process your information based on contractual necessity and legitimate interests.

**If you are located in the EU or UK, this section applies to you.**

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR require us to explain the legal bases we rely on to process your personal information:

- **Performance of a contract.** We process your submissions to deliver the factchecking service you requested by using the app. Using the app constitutes acceptance of this agreement, and processing your submission is necessary to fulfill it.
- **Legitimate interests.** We retain factcheck data to improve the accuracy and quality of our service. We have assessed that this interest does not override your privacy rights, particularly because the data is tied to anonymous ids and does not identify you personally.
- **Legal obligations.** We may process your information where required for compliance with applicable laws.
- **Consent.** Where required by law, we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by contacting us at robointern@reallabs.ai.

**If you are located in Canada, this section applies to you.**

We may process your information if you have given us specific permission (i.e., express consent) to use your personal information for a specific purpose, or in situations where your permission can be inferred (i.e., implied consent). You can withdraw your consent at any time.

In some exceptional cases, we may be legally permitted under applicable law to process your information without your consent, including for example:

- If collection is clearly in the interests of an individual and consent cannot be obtained in a timely way
- For investigations and fraud detection and prevention
- For business transactions provided certain conditions are met
- If disclosure is required to comply with a subpoena, warrant, court order, or rules of the court relating to the production of records
- If the information is publicly available and is specified by the regulations
